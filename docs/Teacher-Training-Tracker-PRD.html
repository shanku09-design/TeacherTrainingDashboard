PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

Teacher Training Tracker

Centralized web-based training planning, delivery, monitoring, and certification

Draft for stakeholder review and business sign-off

Prepared: 15 October 2026 | Baseline sources: Business Requirements v1.0 (30 September 2026) and tender dimensions notes

Audience: Business owner, Schoolnet project owner, technical owner, information security/compliance, implementation and QA teams | Product owner: Product Manager agent

Decision required: Confirm role permissions, training completion and certification rules, personal data, upload contracts, and integration/security behavior before implementation.

1. Executive summary

The Teacher Training Tracker is a centralized, project-scoped web application for planning and managing teacher and student training from project setup through certification and management reporting. It connects projects, training plans, batches, trainers, trainees, day-wise resources and forms, attendance, assessments, feedback, completion, certificates, dashboards, and MIS.

The supplied requirements establish a useful functional baseline but leave consequential rules unresolved. This PRD separates confirmed requirements from decisions that need business, technical, or security approval. Development of the affected workflows should wait for those decisions; work that does not depend on them can proceed.

Product outcomes

Give each authorized role a reliable view of training plans, batches, attendance, participation, progress, and exceptions within its assigned project/location scope.

Reduce scheduling and roster errors through trainer availability, holiday, date, project, plan, capacity, and upload validation.

Make day-wise activity resources, assessments, and feedback available to the right batch participants at the appropriate time.

Provide trustworthy completion and certification outcomes based on approved business rules.

Provide MIS and exports for operational and management decisions while protecting personal information.

3. Product context and users

Project is the primary ownership and access boundary. The business requirement describes the core relationship as Project -> Training Plan -> Batch -> Trainer and Trainees -> Attendance, Resources, Assessments, Feedback -> Completion -> Certification -> Dashboards and MIS. Schoolnet Admin is the stated cross-project exception; other users must see only authorized project and organizational data.

RolePrimary responsibilities in supplied requirements
Schoolnet AdminManage across projects; users, roles, access, training plans, batches, resources, and reports.
ERNET Admin / SponsorMonitor assigned projects and training activities; exact naming and authority need confirmation.
State AdminMonitor training within assigned state/project scope.
School AdminMonitor training associated with their school.
TrainerManage assigned batches and trainee lists; mark attendance; manage permitted resources; review/approve trainee lists; view daily work.
Trainee Teacher / StudentRegister or view own training details, access day-wise resources, complete assessments, submit feedback, view progress and certificate.

Primary journeys

Configure project and master data; create a project-linked training plan with duration and day-linked forms/resources.

Create batches from spreadsheet data; validate project/plan dates, trainer availability, holidays, capacity, and reference data.

Enroll trainees by authorized upload or optional open registration; verify identity using OTP where required; trainer/admin reviews the final roster and closes registration.

Deliver training day by day: assigned trainer records attendance for today or an earlier date, and participants access their scheduled learning materials, assessments, and feedback forms.

Calculate progress and completion using approved rules; issue a configurable certificate only to eligible trainees.

Monitor role-scoped dashboards and MIS, investigate exceptions, export permitted reports, and audit important changes.

4. Scope

Included in the baseline

Project selection/management, with project records sourced from Odoo as described; project dates, code, description, and authorization.

Trainer pool and availability; state/national holiday masters; project/common learning resources; assessment and feedback forms.

Training plan setup, plan types, duration, code, and resource/form assignment by training day.

Batch creation and maintenance primarily through Excel upload, with validation and operational list views.

Trainee roster upload, optional open registration, OTP validation, capacity control, trainer/admin approval, and registration closure.

Daily present/absent attendance capture; assessment and feedback tracking; configurable progress/completion status.

Role-appropriate dashboards and MIS reports, filtering, export, exception views, profile-change audit, and login/resource usage reporting.

Configurable certificate templates with trainee/training details, certificate number, authorized signatory, logos, and color scheme.

Role and access administration, input/file validation, personal-information protection, masking, transport/storage protection, and auditability.

Not specified or requiring separate approval

The source documents do not define implementation technology, hosting architecture, detailed service levels, final release sequencing, complete wireframes, exact personal-data fields, OTP channel/provider, data retention, backup/restore targets, or several operational rules. These are decision items rather than implied scope. Additional integrations or workflows not listed above require stakeholder approval.

6. Business rules and status model

Rule IDConfirmed baseline rule
BR-01Project is the primary data and access boundary; Schoolnet Admin can access all projects.
BR-02Every training plan belongs to a project. Every batch belongs to a project and training plan.
BR-03Training plan code is unique and generated by the system; batch identity uniqueness scope needs confirmation.
BR-04Training plan resources/forms are assigned by day; a day must fall within the plan duration.
BR-05Batch dates must be within project dates; batch size must not exceed approved capacity.
BR-06Trainer availability and applicable holidays are checked when batches are uploaded or edited.
BR-07Registration must be limited to the selected batch and cannot exceed its capacity. Trainer/admin can close it.
BR-08Trainer approval of the trainee list is required before final batch closure.
BR-09Attendance is PRESENT or ABSENT and may be entered for the current or a prior date only. Future dates are disallowed.
BR-10Important changes must be auditable. MIS access/export honors role and project permissions.
BR-11Personal data is masked in MIS; clear trainee name is specified for final certificates. Business must define the exact fields and mask policy.
BR-12Completion/certification thresholds are not approved. The source explicitly leaves attendance percentage and assessment requirements to business.

Confirm transitions, cancellation behavior, and whether statuses are computed or manually managed.

8. Security, privacy, and audit requirements

Role-based access control for all views and actions; enforce project, state, school, batch, trainer assignment, and own-trainee boundaries server-side.

Mask personal information in dashboards, MIS screens, and downloads. Show names in clear text only where approved, including the explicitly required final certificate.

Protect credentials in transit and encrypt data at database level as the source requires. Security architecture must specify key management, encryption coverage, password handling, and access to clear personal fields.

Validate every input and file on the server. Use an approved allowlist for file extensions/types and enforce upload size/content limits once specified.

Audit important changes, attendance corrections, access/role changes, and profile changes. Never expose secrets or clear credentials in audit logs; determine how sensitive old/new values are represented.

Define OTP expiry, delivery, resend and retry controls, failed-login threshold/scope, lockout response and recovery. A 15-minute cool-off is stated; its trigger is not.

Clarify the "do not show clear URL in address bar" requirement. URL obscurity must not substitute for authentication and authorization.

12. Success measures

The product should make the following outcomes measurable in dashboards/MIS. Baselines, exact formulas, targets, and reporting cadence require stakeholder approval; no numeric targets are implied here.

Scheduling reliability: batches passing date, trainer-availability, and holiday validation; number of detected conflicts.

Enrollment control: registrations/uploads accepted, duplicate attempts prevented, capacity exceptions, roster approval completion.

Training delivery: attendance completeness/timeliness, planned vs delivered batches and training days, resource/form access.

Training outcomes: assessment/feedback completion and approved trainee/batch completion/certification rates.

Operational visibility: time to identify unresolved exceptions; report freshness and successful permitted exports.

Privacy/security: masking consistency, authorization test outcomes, audit completeness, and resolved security findings.
